04 • Optic Nerve Preservation

Glaucoma Diagnosis, Laser & MIGS

Because glaucoma damages peripheral optic nerve fibres silently, our institute follows a structured 3-stage pressure-lowering protocol tailored to your target intraocular pressure.

Stage 01 • Diagnostics

RNFL OCT & Visual Field Mapping

Combines Goldmann applanation tonometry, corneal pachymetry, 360° gonioscopy, and Humphrey automated perimetry to establish your baseline nerve reserve.

Stage 02 • Laser Therapy

SLT & YAG Laser Iridotomy

Selective Laser Trabeculoplasty (SLT) gently enhances natural trabecular meshwork drainage in a 5-minute office visit—often eliminating daily eye drop fatigue.

Stage 03 • Micro-Stents

MIGS & Filtration Surgery

Minimally Invasive Glaucoma Surgery (iStent / Hydrus micro-stents) implanted during cataract surgery, plus mitomycin-C trabeculectomy and drainage valves.

07 • Preventive Diagnostics

Comprehensive 6-Point Eye Examination

A structured 60-minute diagnostic workup recommended annually for adults over 40, diabetics, and high-myopia patients.

01
Step 1 • 10 Mins

Digital Refraction & Acuity

Wavefront autorefraction and subjective phoropter testing for distance, computer, and reading prescriptions.

02
Step 2 • 10 Mins

Slit-Lamp Biomicroscopy

High-magnification optical sectioning of the eyelids, tear film, corneal endothelium, iris, and crystalline lens.

03
Step 3 • 5 Mins

Goldmann IOP & Pachymetry

Intraocular pressure calibrated against central corneal thickness to screen accurately for ocular hypertension.

04
Step 4 • 15 Mins

Dilated Peripheral Fundus

Stereoscopic inspection of the optic disc cup-to-disc ratio, retinal vessels, and 360° peripheral retina.

05
Step 5 • 10 Mins

Spectral-Domain OCT Scan

5-micron cross-sectional tomography of the macula and retinal nerve fibre layer (RNFL).

06
Step 6 • 10 Mins

Ophthalmologist Review

1-on-1 discussion of your imaging results with a printed clinical summary and personalized preventive plan.
