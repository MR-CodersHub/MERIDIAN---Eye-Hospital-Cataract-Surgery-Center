During cataract surgery, your clouded natural crystalline lens is gently replaced with a clear, biocompatible artificial lens called an Intraocular Lens (IOL). Selecting the right IOL is one of the most important decisions you will make with your surgeon.

1. Monofocal Aspheric IOLs

Monofocal lenses provide crisp, high-contrast vision at a single focal distanceâ€”typically set for clear distance vision (driving, television). Reading glasses are needed for close-up print, and they remain the gold standard for patients with retinal or optic nerve conditions.

2. Toric IOLs for Astigmatism

If corneal topography shows regular astigmatism, a Toric IOL neutralizes that curvature inside the eye, eliminating blurred or shadowed edges at distance.

3. Trifocal & Extended Depth of Focus (EDOF) IOLs

Designed for patients seeking broad freedom from spectacles across distance, intermediate (computer), and near reading. Candidacy requires a healthy macula, stable tear film, and realistic counseling regarding mild night-time halos during neural adaptation.

Cataracts rarely cause sudden blindness; instead, they gradually scatter incoming light. Common early signs include halos around oncoming headlights, faded colour vibrancy, and needing brighter lamps to read.

Modern micro-incision phacoemulsification is actually safer and requires less ultrasound energy when performed before the lens becomes overly dense. Schedule a diagnostic evaluation whenever visual haze interferes with your daily confidence.

Before approving any patient for Contoura LASIK or SMILE, our refractive team verifies five non-negotiable criteria: (1) Prescription stability for at least 12 months, (2) Adequate residual stromal bed thickness on Pentacam tomography, (3) Symmetrical corneal curvature free of keratoconus, (4) Healthy tear-film break-up time, and (5) Absence of uncontrolled autoimmune disease.

Remember to pause soft contact lenses for 7 days prior to your screening appointment so your cornea returns to its natural curvature.

Elevated blood sugar gradually weakens tiny retinal capillaries, leading to microscopic fluid leakage or abnormal vessel growth. Crucially, early diabetic macular oedema can be completely painless and asymptomatic.

Annual dilated fundus exams paired with Spectral-Domain OCT allow us to detect sub-clinical swelling early and stabilize vision with targeted Anti-VEGF micro-therapy.

Glaucoma damages the optic nerve fibres responsible for peripheral vision first, which is why central 20/20 vision can remain intact until advanced stages. Even individuals with "normal" intraocular pressure can develop normal-tension glaucoma.

Baseline RNFL OCT imaging and automated visual field testing after age 40 provide an objective benchmark to protect your sight for decades.

Global ophthalmic studies demonstrate that 90 to 120 minutes of daily outdoor daylight stimulates retinal dopamine release, helping regulate healthy eyeball growth and slowing childhood myopia progression.

For children whose prescription increases rapidly each year, Meridian offers evidence-based myopia control including low-dose atropine drops and peripheral-defocus spectacle lenses.
